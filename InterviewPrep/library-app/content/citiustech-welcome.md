This track is built from the CitiusTech job description and the resume you submitted. Its chapters come from the shared library; the [track page](#/track/citiustech) lists them by priority. The role is titled **Full Stack Python Developer**, but the job description reads like a **Python & AI Engineer**. The mandatory skills are **Python, React and Healthcare**, and the responsibilities add building, training and deploying ML models. The recruiter said the round is either **30% coding and 60% technical questions**, or **60% coding** with technical questions for the rest. So you need to be sharp at live coding in Python, and you need clear, confident answers across the stack.

## What the interview will test

| Area | Why it is asked | Your position |
|---|---|---|
| **Live coding in Python** | 30–60% of the round | Practise out loud tonight: it is the biggest single block |
| **Core Python** | Mandatory skill | Strong; refresh the classic questions (GIL, decorators, generators, mutability) |
| **FastAPI, Pydantic, SQL** | "Scalable applications using Python" | Strong: your LDH Medicaid Document AI backend |
| **React** | Mandatory skill | Strong: myMedidata, Labels Web, LINE |
| **Healthcare** | Mandatory skill | Your differentiator: Molina, Medidata, LDH Medicaid, Labcorp |
| **Machine learning** | "Build, train, evaluate and deploy models" | The gap: learn the vocabulary and the reasoning |
| **GenAI** | AI-driven solutions in production | Strong: Document AI, structured output, agentic workflows |
| **Platform and design** | Production-grade enterprise systems | Strong: AKS, Cloud Run, CI/CD |

## How each chapter works

- **Notes** at the top are open: read them quickly.
- **Interview questions** hide the answer. Say your answer **out loud** first, as if the interviewer just asked, then press **Show answer**. The first paragraph of every answer is the version to say in 20–40 seconds; the rest is depth for follow-ups.
- **Must know** marks the questions most likely tomorrow. Tick **Must know only** at the top of a question list to see just those.
- **Coding problems** show the statement. Solve it in a plain editor or on paper first, take hints one at a time, then compare with the solution. Every solution in the library has been run, with tests.
- **Rate yourself** on every card: Nailed it, Shaky or Missed. The [Weak spots](#/progress) page collects everything shaky or missed, so tomorrow morning you revise only those.

## The day of the interview

- **Set up 15 minutes early:** camera, microphone, a quiet room, water, and a plain-text editor and Python 3 open in case they let you run code.
- **Expect a shared editor** such as HackerRank, CoderPad or a Google Doc. It may have no autocomplete and may not run code, so practise writing code without running it.
- **Talk the whole time** in the coding part. Silence is the worst signal; a clear plan with a small bug beats a silent perfect answer.
- **Lead with healthcare plus AI** in your introduction: it is what makes you different from other Python developers.
- **Have your questions ready.** Ask how much of the work is model training versus full-stack Python and React (the [stories chapter](#/citiustech-stories) has a list).
- **Rate:** quote only the rate already agreed in your Right to Represent with the recruiter.
